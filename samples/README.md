# Sample save files

Save files for trying Save Stats without a game of your own.

| File | Game | Trainer | Party | Caught |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `red.sav` | Pokémon Red | A | 2 | 5 |
| `yellow-1.srm` | Pokémon Yellow (randomized) | YELLOW | 5 | 7 |
| `yellow-2.srm` | Pokémon Yellow (randomized) | A | 5 | 5 |
| `yellow-3.srm` | Pokémon Yellow (randomized) | YELLOW | 1 | 1 |

The files are copied unchanged from the test saves of the
[savechecker module](https://github.com/Akistolol88/Pokemon-Red-Blue-Savechecker-Module)
(`savechecker/src/test/resources/saves/`), which is released under the Unlicense.

They come from different games, not from one game saved at different times, so the charts of
progress between saves work but do not show real progress.
